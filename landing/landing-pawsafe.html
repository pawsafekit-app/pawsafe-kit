The honest questions, answered

Is my purchase secure?

Yes. Payment is processed through a secure, encrypted checkout, and we never see or store your card details.

What paper should I use?

Standard A4 paper on any regular inkjet or laser printer. If you use Letter, just choose “fit to page” in your printer settings.

When do I get access?

Immediately after you register your pet. You'll receive the documents by email, or you can download them any time from your pet panel.

Do the documents have legal value?

No. The Pet ID and the Birth Certificate are personalized documents designed to make vet forms and check-ins easier. They are not government-issued and have no legal value.

How are the documents delivered?

They're available in your account on the site. You'll be guided step by step to fill them in, print them and get them ready.

How does the ID tag work?

After registering your pet, you get tag templates in several sizes, each with a QR code to cut out and attach to the collar. When someone scans it with their phone, a page opens with your pet's info and your contact details — and, if the finder allows it, the scan location is sent to your phone. It's a QR tag, not a GPS tracker.

What if it's not for me?

Just send us an email within 7 days and we'll refund you in full, no forms and no questions.
