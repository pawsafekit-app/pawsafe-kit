PawSafe Family Pack

Add up to 5 more pets to your kit. Each one gets its own documents and its own QR tag, and you can give the whole family a matching design.

Your pets

Adding a pet opens your kit on the new pet so you can fill in the details and photo. Owner name, phone and address are copied from Pet 1 (you can change them).

Matching design

When on, every pet uses Pet 1's kit color and Studio style. Names, photos and QR codes stay different.

Family color

This sets Pet 1's color. For templates, fonts and backgrounds, open PawSafe Studio on Pet 1.

Family QR tags

One sheet with 2 tag pairs per pet. Each QR opens that pet's own page.
