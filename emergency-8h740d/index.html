About this emergency

The QR opens a page where the finder can call you or send their location on WhatsApp. That page includes your phone number, so anyone who scans the QR can see it. This is not GPS: you are only notified if the finder chooses to message you.

Free to use. Posts are public: share only what you are comfortable making public. Images are 1080 px wide: post 1:1 (Instagram, Facebook) and story 9:16.
